## Statement

![The monkey archivist and his jungle storeroom.](/files/66bb612186716af0222a62fb59a5d742/heap-of-secrets.jpg)

Deep in the jungle, a monkey built a storeroom for things he promised to keep secret. He ran out of drawers before lunch, so he emptied a few and started using them again.

He kept the old bookmarks, of course. Now someone's bookmark opens a vault where there used to be a shopping list. The monkey insists his reels have the whole story.

Can you work out which drawer he reused before he eats another stamp?

Connect to the competition instance and type `help` at the storeroom console. Work from one session's archive at a time.

Difficulty: Monkey

Architecture: Linux x86-64
