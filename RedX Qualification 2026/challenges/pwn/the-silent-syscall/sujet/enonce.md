## Statement

![The monkey DJ at his jungle silent disco.](/files/e56e8491298070eca74ca6b679697131/the-silent-syscall.jpg)

Deep in the jungle, a monkey opened a silent disco. He handed out headphones, dimmed the lights, and announced the next track.

Then all three booths lost sync. The headphones went quiet. The monkey tried to restore the show from his old cue cards, but a stage rule blocked one of the channels.

The crowd thinks this is part of the set. Can you find the cue that would have brought the music back?

Connect to the competition instance and type `help` at the DJ's console. Use the clock marks and cards from the same session.
