## Statement

![The monkey examiner sorting two lanes of punch cards.](/files/4953f10dfe293ce81129e19b9a1c1d41/the-last-calling-convention.jpg)

The jungle's calling convention examiner has promoted himself to chief examiner. He has a bow tie, two rows of punch cards, and nine recordings of calls he claims went perfectly.

One card comes as a pair. He tried giving half of it to one register and half to the stack. On another call, he gave back somebody else's register marker. He insists both deserve full marks.

Can you grade the right recordings before he eats your answer sheet?

Run `./the-last-calling-convention` on Linux x86-64 for a practice session. Connect to the competition instance for a scored session. Type `help` in either console.
