## Statement

![A monkey pulls paper tape from an old jungle relay terminal.](/files/cd27124d0d8e98b9812e016f344abacf/broken-boundaries.jpg)

Deep in the jungle, a monkey found a relay station and made himself chief of dispatch.

His first shift went well, right up until he moved a boundary, canceled a shipment, and heard the wrong alarm go off.

The monkey swears the receipt proves his innocence. He also signed it with a banana. Can you work out what the relay actually filed before he eats the paperwork?

Open the challenge instance during the competition and type `help` when the relay console appears. Keep your notes with the session that produced them.
