## Statement

into `epitaph.html` and the stone carves it, followed by its verdict: `REJECTED`... or `ACCEPTED`.

There's no server, no password check, no JavaScript doing the judging. Open the page's source if you
like. The only thing deciding your fate is `reaper.ttf`.

Find the one name the stone accepts.
