# Ashes in the Water

- **Category:** Osint
- **Author:** Shadowmonarch
- **Difficulty:** easy
- **Points:** 100 (dynamic, initial 500)
- **Solves:** 175
- **Instance:** none

## Statement

New England, 1892. "Consumption" is wiping out a farming family one by one: first the mother, then the eldest daughter, then a 19-year-old girl buried in the dead of winter. Now the **only son** is coughing blood.

The neighbours whisper that one of the dead isn't resting. On a cold March day, the men of the village go to the family plot with shovels. Two of the bodies are just what you'd expect. The third, two months in the ground, still has **blood in her heart**.

They cut the heart out, burn it on a nearby rock, stir the ashes into water, and give it to the dying brother to drink.

The Reaper wants three things for the ledger:
1. The **town** where this happened
2. The **first name** of the brother who drank the ashes
3. The **exact date** the bodies were dug up

**Flag format:** `RxR{Town_FirstName_DDMMYYYY}`

Example: `RxR{Salem_John_31101700}`
