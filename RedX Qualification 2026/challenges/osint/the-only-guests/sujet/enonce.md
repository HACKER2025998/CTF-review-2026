# The Only Guests

- **Category:** Osint
- **Author:** Shadowmonarch
- **Difficulty:** easy
- **Points:** 100 (dynamic, initial 500)
- **Solves:** 265
- **Instance:** none

## Statement

Autumn, 1974. A young horror writer and his wife drive up a mountain road in Colorado and check into a grand old hotel on the very last night of the season. Tomorrow it closes for winter. Tonight, they are the **only guests**.

The corridors are long and empty. That night, he has a nightmare. By morning, he has the bones of one of the most famous horror novels ever written.

The hotel still stands, and one room is booked months ahead by people hoping something knocks back.

**Find the hotel and the number of the room the couple slept in.**

**Flag format:** `RxR{HotelName_RoomNumber}`. Use one word for the hotel name and leave out the word "Hotel".

Example: `RxR{Ritz_101}`
