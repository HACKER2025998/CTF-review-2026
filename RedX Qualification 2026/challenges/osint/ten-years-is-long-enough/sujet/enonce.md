# Ten Years Is Long Enough

- **Category:** Osint
- **Author:** Shadowmonarch
- **Difficulty:** easy
- **Points:** 100 (dynamic, initial 500)
- **Solves:** 250
- **Instance:** none

## Statement

They called him the man no lock could hold. Chains, straitjackets, sealed water tanks: he walked out of all of them. But he couldn't escape **Halloween night**.

Before he died, he and his wife made a pact. If the dead can speak, he would send her a secret message that no fake medium could ever guess. Every Halloween, she waited.

The Reaper found this scrap of paper inside an old séance cabinet:

```
answer · tell · pray · answer · look · tell · answer · answer · tell
```
On the tenth Halloween, she gave him one last chance on a **rooftop**, with the hills behind her. Then she put out the candle for good.

**Find the full secret message and the hotel where the last attempt was held.**

**Flag format:** `RxR{HotelName_Word1_Word2}`. Use one distinctive word for the hotel name, with no "Hotel" and no city name.
