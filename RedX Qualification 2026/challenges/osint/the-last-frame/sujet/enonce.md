# The Last Frame

- **Category:** Osint
- **Author:** Shadowmonarch
- **Difficulty:** —
- **Points:** 419 (dynamic, initial 500)
- **Solves:** 10
- **Instance:** none

## Statement

Nobody knew his real name. To his followers he was just **@ghostframe96**, a solo photographer who collected the world's most unsettling places the way other people collect fridge magnets.

A diamond town swallowed by desert sand. A village abandoned on the edge of a cliff. A city frozen in time since 1986. A concrete island left to the sea. Wherever people were afraid to go, he went, camera first.

His followers were used to his silences. He would vanish for weeks, then come back with one haunting photograph and a cryptic caption that only made sense later.

**This time, he never came back.**

It has been almost three weeks. No new posts. No replies. His phone goes straight to voicemail, and his hotel room was found untouched. The investigators have run out of leads, so the case is now yours.

The last trace of him is a single post, dated in his own words: **07.11.26**. A fortress of dark stone, its Gothic towers burning gold in the late-afternoon sun. No geotag. No tagged accounts. Just a caption, and then nothing.

Everyone leaves pieces of themselves online, scattered across platforms they forgot they ever joined. Find those pieces, put him back together, and work out exactly where he was standing when the trail went cold.

---

###  Your Mission

Find out:
1. Where was he standing when his last photo was taken?
2. What is his real first name?
3. When was he born?
4. What did he study?
5. What does he do for a living?
6. What does he do when he isn't exploring abandoned places?

---

###  Flag Format

Combine all six answers in order. No spaces, each word capitalized, date as DD-MM-YYYY:

`RxR{Location_FirstName_DD-MM-YYYY_Major_Job_Hobby}`
