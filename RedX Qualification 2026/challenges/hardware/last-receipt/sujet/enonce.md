## Statement

Old Mr. Grim of *Grim & Sons Funeral Supply* died at his desk, mid-sentence. The family is sure he
wrote down his last words somewhere. The only thing on his desk was the shop's **receipt printer**.

It's still on the network. It's a raw network printer: it doesn't log in, it doesn't greet you, it
just **prints whatever you send it**. The paper comes back to you as text. Try it: connect and type a line.

Receipt printers don't speak English, though. They speak **ESC/POS**, a language of control bytes.
Ask the printer what it is. Then ask it what it remembers.
