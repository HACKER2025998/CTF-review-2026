## Statement

The old tomb isn't locked with a key. It's locked with a **tone**. Its *spirit lock* listens on one wire
and opens only when it hears a square wave at one exact frequency and duty cycle. The display on the lock
tells you which.

On the bench: a breadboard, a 9 V battery, a lone **NE555** timer chip, and a drawer of parts with no
labels. Just colour bands and tiny printed codes.

Build the oscillator. Wire it right. Make the tomb sing.

Careful: the dead don't sell spare chips. (Well, they do. But you'll see the smoke.)
