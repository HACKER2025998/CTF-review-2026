## Statement

The Vane family vault was sealed in 1931 by a man who didn't trust locksmiths. There's no keyhole. The
door bolt is driven by a solenoid, and the solenoid answers to a cabinet of **relays**: four toggle
switches, two push buttons, a row of clicking coils, an alarm bell, and a timer relay that takes its time.

The original **wiring sheet** is pinned inside the cabinet door. Someone spilled coffee on it.

Read the ladder. Throw the switches in the right order. Don't wake the bell.
